Questions

Before you upgrade.

Will I lose my resume if I downgrade later?

No. Every resume you've built stays in your account. If you drop back to Free, you'll simply be limited to editing and downloading one of them at a time.

What counts as a "chatbot message"?

Each message you send to the resume builder chatbot counts toward your monthly limit on Free. Premium removes this cap entirely, so you can iterate as many times as you like.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Premium is month-to-month or annual with no lock-in. Cancel from your account settings and you'll keep Premium access until the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer refunds?

If Premium isn't working for you, reach out within 7 days of your first charge and we'll refund it in full — no forms, no back-and-forth.
